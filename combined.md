M O D E L S ,  O W N E R S H I P  &  T H E  L A W
Why Indian Marketplaces Look the Way They Do
Press Note 3 (2016): 100% FDI allowed in the marketplace model. FDI in the inventory model prohibited
Press Note 2 (2018, effective Feb 2019): no vendor the marketplace holds equity in may sell on it. If more than 25% of a vendor's 
purchases come from the marketplace group, the marketplace is treated as controlling that inventory
Also under PN2: no forced exclusivity; cashbacks must be fair and non-discriminatory
Still in force in 2026
A foreign-owned Amazon or Flipkart cannot own the stock it sells in India.
