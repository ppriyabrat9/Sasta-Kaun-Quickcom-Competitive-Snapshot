Sasta Kaun?

psst… it's not who you think

Same grocery list. Six 10-minute apps. Ten cities. I set each app to the same PIN code and priced 28 everyday items, from aloo to atta.

01 · Why this exists

Every app claims it's cheapest. Nobody shows the receipt.

Why

Quick commerce is now ~70% of India's online grocery. People pick an app by habit, not price.

The bet

Apps don't fight on branded packs (the MRP is printed on them). They fight on sabzi, where nobody can compare.

What I did

Same PIN, same 28 items, every app, one evening. Shelf price only, no coupons, no fees.
